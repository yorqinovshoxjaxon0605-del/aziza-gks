01 / About the Artist

Observation. Expression. Imagination.

My name is Jovburieva Aziza Gayrat Kizi. I am an artist and a candidate for the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS).

My work includes graphite studies of classical plaster heads, portraits, narrative illustrations, and oil painting. This selection brings together eleven works created between 2020 and 2026, spanning studies of form and light as well as scenes of personal connection.

Alongside my artistic practice, my achievements include first place at the regional stage of a design competition and recognition for class leadership.
